Accessibility Statement

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Our Commitment

Headroom Advisors is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We are actively working to increase the accessibility and usability of our website and, in doing so, adhere to many of the available standards and guidelines.

Conformance Standard

This website aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and to meet the requirements of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities and more usable for everyone.

Accessibility Features

  • Semantic HTML structure to support screen readers and assistive technologies
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background surfaces
  • Keyboard-navigable menus, links, and form controls
  • Descriptive labels on all form fields
  • Responsive layout that adapts to assistive zoom and varied screen sizes
  • Reduced-motion consideration for users sensitive to animation

Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We periodically review our website against WCAG success criteria and make improvements as we identify opportunities, including as we add new content or features.

Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some content or functionality may not yet be fully accessible. If you encounter a barrier, please let us know so we can address it.

Feedback and Contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of headroomadvisors.com. If you experience any difficulty accessing any part of this website, or would like to request information in an alternative format, please reach out to us through the application form on our Apply page and describe the issue. We will make reasonable efforts to respond and provide the information, item, or transaction through an alternative communication method.
