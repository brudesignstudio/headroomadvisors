Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Overview

Headroom Advisors ("Headroom," "we," "us," or "our") provides fractional COO and operational advisory services to growth-stage companies. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through headroomadvisors.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including when you submit an application or inquiry through our contact form:

  • Contact details, name, email address, company name
  • Business information, industry, annual revenue range, service of interest
  • Application content, the operational challenges and goals you describe to us

We also automatically collect limited technical information when you visit the Site, such as browser type, device type, and general usage patterns, through standard analytics tools.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To evaluate and respond to applications for advisory sessions, project engagements, and Fractional COO relationships
  • To communicate with you about your application or an active engagement
  • To improve the Site and our services
  • To comply with legal obligations

Applications are reviewed personally by our co-founders. We do not add applicants to a marketing mailing list or send unsolicited follow-up offers.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who support our operations (such as scheduling, email, or hosting providers), each bound by confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

5. With Your Permission, Case Studies

If you complete a founding client session, we may ask for your permission to feature an anonymized version of your engagement as a case study on our podcast or content channels. We will never do so without your explicit, separate consent.

6. Data Retention

We retain application and engagement information for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the email address below. We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Security

We use commercially reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to us through the application form on our Apply page.
